Common questions

What is the minimum order quantity?

500 pieces per style for woven garments and 300 pieces per style for knitwear.

Which garments do you manufacture?

Woven shirts, pants, dresses, blouses, nightwear and loungewear, and knitted t-shirts, polos, hoodies, sweatshirts, joggers and athleisure, for men, women and kids.

Do you offer private label and embellishment?

Yes - your labels, tags and care content, plus embroidery, prints and garment washes, and packaging to your carton spec.

Can you make a style that is not listed?

With a tech pack we can make virtually any knitted or woven garment that is technically feasible. Send the spec and we will say honestly whether it fits.
