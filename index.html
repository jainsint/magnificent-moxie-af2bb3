Common questions

What is the minimum order quantity?

500 pieces per style for woven garments and 300 pieces per style for knitwear.

What are the lead times?

Samples in 10 to 14 days, and production in 60 to 90 days from an approved sample.

Where do you ship?

India, the United Kingdom, the United States, Australia, Singapore and Malaysia. Export documentation is prepared in house.

What do you manufacture?

Woven shirts, pants, dresses and blouses, and knitted t-shirts, polos, hoodies, joggers and athleisure, for men, women and kids - including medical apparel and corporate uniforms.
